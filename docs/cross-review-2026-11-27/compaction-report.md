| 1   | **defect** (lost data by a late commit) | `commitCompaction`'s exact-inputs check compares sorted-run **ids**, and the scheduler reuses them. So does a commit step that Workflows retries after 16 other commits have pushed its id out of `recentCompactionJobIds` (D103 keeps 16). | 2   | **risk + docs**                         | A job that reads `Running` for ever pins D96's collection cap. `RUNNING_STATUSES` counts `queued`, `paused`, `waiting` and `waitingForPause` as running. Every poll renews such a job (`lastProgressAt`, its lease), and D94's bound covers only unreadable jobs. So a paused Workflow instance (an operator's action) or a queue that never drains keeps `runCollection`'s `oldestRunning` at its start for good. Nothing written after that start is collected, `gcSstBoundary` stops moving, and the job's sources and budget lease stay taken. D96 says "D94 bounds how long a job can be recorded as running", and `verify-gc-safety`'s D96 note says the same; that is wrong for these statuses. | `tests/compactor-running-forever.snippet.ts` (append to `compactor.test.ts`; fails today): a job that reads `Running` for 7 days still caps the collection at its start.                                                                                                                                                                | A job recorded as running longer than a maximum age (say 6 h; D97 bounds a real job's steps far below that) is logged and finished like an unseen one: lease released, cap lifted. Its late commit is then refused by the inputs check, once finding 1 is fixed. `health` reports a job past the age.                                                     |

## Stored data (D105)

- Compactor state `lastProgressAt` (D94): older code drops it, and a job's unseen bound falls back
  to `startedAt`. Safe.
- Manifest `recentCompactionJobIds` (D103): older code drops it on its next commit. A commit
  retried across a rollback is then not recognized and falls back to the inputs check, which is
  finding 1's path. Safe once finding 1 is fixed.
